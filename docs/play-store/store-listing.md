## Full description (max 4000)

Forensim is a forensic science learning app for college students and their professors. Study the
theory, then walk into a 3D crime scene and practise doing it right.

INVESTIGATE IN 3D
• Walk through realistic crime scenes: apartments, offices, garages, stores and more
• Mark, photograph and collect evidence in the proper order, with the right tools
• Every case is generated by your professor, with a code or QR to join
• Get scored on procedure: documentation, collection, packaging and chain of custody

SEE EVIDENCE IN AR
• Place bullets, casings, weapons and other evidence on your desk and study them up close

LEARN THE SUBJECTS
• Photography, personal identification, chemistry and toxicology, questioned documents,
  polygraph, ballistics and crime scene investigation
• Lessons with images, key terms and offline reading
• Practice quizzes and graded quizzes from your professor

A LITTLE EVERY DAY
• Forensic term of the day
• Quiz of the day: one question, one try. Keep your 🔥 streak going
• Evidence of the day: identify the exhibit in the photo

AI TUTOR
• Ask questions about your lessons and get clear explanations
• Locked during graded quizzes and cases

FOR PROFESSORS
• Create classes, approve students and chat with your class
• Publish quizzes and generate crime-scene cases
• See every student's scores and download them as a spreadsheet (CSV)

Available in English and Filipino. Forensim is a training simulation for education; it is not a
tool for processing real evidence.
