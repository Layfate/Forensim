Forensim · Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Forensim account and the data linked to it at any time. Choose either option below.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Forensim and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings > Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request by email

If you can't use the app, email us from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Forensim account". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days, then reply to let you know.

Email a deletion request

Or write to elorde.princeart6881@gmail.com.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, profile (name, gender, student ID) and profile photo
  • Your class memberships and join requests
  • Your lesson progress, quiz and case attempts, answers and scores
  • Your class chat messages
  • Your daily quiz and evidence answers and your streak
  • Your AI tutor usage records

What is kept

  • Classes, lessons, quizzes and cases you created for other students are kept, without your name, so those students don't lose their coursework.
  • Audit records of administrative actions are kept for accountability with your identity removed. If a school administrator removed your account, that record keeps your account's email address.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how Forensim handles your data.
